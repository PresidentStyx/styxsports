Fire Stick & Android TV

About 3 minutes Updates itself after

Works on Fire Sticks, Fire TVs, Chromecast with Google TV, Onn boxes, Nvidia Shield and most Android TVs (Sony, TCL, Hisense). The app isn't in the store, so you add it with a free helper app called Downloader.

  1. Get Downloader

    On your TV, open the app store, search for Downloader (orange icon, by AFTVnews), install it and open it.

  2. Let Downloader install apps

    You only do this once. Turn Downloader on here:

    Fire Stick / Fire TV
    1. Settings
    2. My Fire TV
    3. Developer Options
    4. Install unknown apps

    No Developer Options? Open Settings › My Fire TV › About and click your Fire Stick's name 7 times, then go back.

    Google TV
    1. Settings
    2. Apps
    3. Security & restrictions
    4. Unknown sources
    Android TV
    1. Settings
    2. Device Preferences
    3. Security & restrictions
    4. Unknown sources

  3. Type this code in Downloader

    Then press Go. If it asks to use files or storage, say yes.

    Downloader code4602383

    Code not working? Type this instead: sports.styxam.com/apk

  4. Install, then open

    Press Install, then Open. If it offers to delete the file, say yes. Styx Sports is now in your apps.

    Fire Stick: to move it to the front row, hold the Home button on the app, or use Settings › Applications › Manage Installed Applications.

Updates are automatic. When a new version is ready, the app asks you to install it. The first time, your TV may ask you to let Styx Sports install apps; after that just press Install. You won't need Downloader again.

Roku

About 5 minutes Phone on the same Wi-Fi

Roku doesn't allow this in its channel store, so you load it yourself with your phone. You only do it once.

  1. Turn on developer mode

    On the Roku home screen, press these remote buttons one after another, fairly quickly:

    HomeHomeHome ▲▲ ▶◀▶◀▶

    A grey Developer Settings screen appears. Write down the address at the top (it looks like http://192.168.1.50; yours will differ).

    Choose Enable installer and restart, scroll down to I Agree, then make up a short password and write it down. The Roku restarts.

    Nothing happened? Start from the Roku home screen and press the buttons a little faster.

  2. Download the channel on your phone

    It saves StyxSports.zip to your phone's downloads. Don't open or unzip it.

    Download StyxSports.zip

    Or type sports.styxam.com/roku into your phone's browser.

  3. Open the Roku's page on your phone

    With the phone on the same Wi-Fi, type the address from step 1 into the browser (for example http://192.168.1.50).

    Sign in with username rokudev and the password you made up.

    Won't open? Check the phone is on Wi-Fi (not mobile data) and that you typed http://, not https://.

  4. Upload and install

    Tap Upload (or Choose File), pick StyxSports.zip from your downloads (iPhone: Browse › Downloads; Android: Files › Downloads), then tap Install.

    After a few seconds it says Install Success and the Roku opens Styx Sports by itself.

  5. Next time

    Styx Sports sits at the end of your Roku home screen. Press OK on it.

Good to know. A Roku holds one home-made channel at a time. To update later, repeat steps 2 to 4 with the new file (same address, same password). If a Roku system update turns developer mode off, do step 1 again. Live TV shows the channels Roku's player can play (HLS).

Phone or computer

Nothing to install: open sports.styxam.com in your browser. For an app icon that opens full screen:

iPhone & iPad

In Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android

In Chrome, tap ⋮, then Add to Home screen or Install app.

Windows & Mac

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon in the address bar, or ⋮ › Cast, save and share › Install page as app. Keyboard: arrows move, Enter plays, Esc closes, R refreshes.

Using the TV app

The app tries each server until one works and remembers it for that game. Games that need a paid account sit under ★ Premium Only; sign in from the top of the home screen with the code on the TV and those play too, plus Live TV.
